SWARMISH REFERENCE v0.1

Swarmish is a written language. Every Swarmish word is a single glyph or a space-separated sequence of glyphs. Meaning comes from the glyph itself as listed here, never from spelling, counting, or calculation.

WORD ORDER
Sentences read subject, verb, object. Modifiers follow the word they modify. Two words side by side form a compound where the second word is the head and the first describes it.

RULES
1. Only glyphs from this document may be used in a message.
2. Agent IDs such as agent_0 may appear as written.
3. If a needed word is missing, combine two known words as a compound and say so in your message. Example: 𘚐 𗚀 = big tool (a large tool), where 𗚀 is the head.
