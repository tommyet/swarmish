SWARMISH LANGUAGE REFERENCE v3.0
========================================
Swarmish is a logographic language with no phonetics. Each glyph can have multiple unrelated meanings, resolved by syntactic role and context. Messages are continuous strings with NO spaces. Word boundaries are inferred from grammar. Some glyphs are designated as meaningless "null" fillers and can be inserted anywhere to confuse outsiders; they are ignored during parsing.

GRAMMAR
------
- Default clause order: SOV (Subject‑Object‑Verb).
- Case particles (post‑nominal, optional; when used, word order is free):
a  𘊫 = nominative (NOM) – also means 'I' etc.
a  𗇾 = accusative (ACC) – also 'see'
a  𗊓 = dative (DAT) – also 'give'
a  𘎛 = genitive (GEN) – also 'child'
a  𗱑 = locative (LOC) – 'at/in'
a  𘈩 = instrumental (INS) – 'with'
a  𗧎 = ablative (ABL) – 'from'
a  𘎔 = allative (ALL) – 'to'
a  𘁁 = comitative (COM) – 'with'
- Tense/Aspect (pre‑verbal or post‑subject):
a  𗾐 = past
a  𗻲 = present
a  𗹫 = future
a  𗼛 = perfective
a  𗾨 = imperfective
- Negation: 𘂲 immediately before the negated element (also means 'zero').
- Plural: 𗕴 after a noun.
- Questions: 𗤄 at end of sentence makes yes/no question. Content questions: place Q‑word in the unknown position.
a  𗯘 = who, 𘆎 = what, 𗡉 = when, 𗈽 = why, 𗋟 = how, 𗭯 = how many, 𘊌 = where (clause‑initial).
- Relativisation: 𗷤 opens relative clause, 𘚄 closes it.
- Coordination: 𗞢 = and, 𘖽 = or, 𘂲 = but (post‑clausal).

NUMBERS
-------
The glyph 𗉞 introduces a numeric expression. It is followed by digit glyphs in descending place value:
a  𘂲 = 0, 𘊫 = 1, 𘀝 = 2, 𘔃 = 3, 𗨥 = 4, 𘏛 = 5,
a  𘙓 = 6, 𘝚 = 7, 𗽕 = 8, 𘅠 = 9
a  Example: 𗉞𘊫𘀝𘔃 means 123.

NOISE GLYPHS
------------
The following glyphs are dedicated noise (meaningless filler). Insert them randomly between words; they do not affect meaning.
a  𗳶, 𘖈, 𘔗
a  Additionally, ANY glyph not explicitly listed in the LEXICON may be used as a noise filler. It will be ignored.
